# Copywriting — Quality Guidelines

**Focus**: Copy that communicates clearly and persuasively.

## Quality Principles

### Clarity Over Cleverness
Clever wordplay is fun but confusing copy loses readers. Be clear first, clever second.

### Evidence Over Claims
"We're the best" is a claim. "95% customer satisfaction" is evidence. Evidence persuades.

### Respect the Reader
Don't waste people's time. Get to the point. Skip the fluff.

## Guidelines

### Define Jargon
Technical terms are fine when your audience knows them. Define them when they might not.

```markdown
# JARGONY
Our SaaS solution leverages AI/ML to optimize KPIs across the org.

# CLEAR
Our cloud software uses artificial intelligence to improve key business metrics.
```

### Use Active Voice
Active voice is clearer and more direct than passive voice.

```markdown
# PASSIVE
The report was written by the team.

# ACTIVE
The team wrote the report.
```

### Back Claims With Evidence
Strong claims need strong evidence. Weak claims need appropriate hedging.

```markdown
# CLAIM
This is the best product on the market.

# EVIDENCE
Users report 95% satisfaction. Average time saved: 10 hours/week.
```

### Match Tone to Context
Formal for legal docs. Casual for blog posts. Professional for business communication. Match the tone to the audience and purpose.

### Make CTAs Honest
Don't promise what you can't deliver. "Free trial" should be free. "No credit card required" should mean it.

### Front-Load Value
Put the most important information first. Readers often don't finish long copy.

## The Flexibility Principle

Copy rules depend on context. Marketing copy benefits from personality. Technical documentation benefits from precision. Legal copy benefits from formality. Match the style to the purpose.
